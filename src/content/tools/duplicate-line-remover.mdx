## What it does

Paste a list, get the same list without duplicates — with live stats on how many lines came in, went out, and were removed. Trimming, case-insensitive comparison, empty-line removal, and optional sorting are all one checkbox away.

## Examples

**Merging two email lists.** Concatenate both exports, paste, enable TRIM and IGNORE CASE. `alpha@example.com` and `  Alpha@example.com ` collapse into one entry, and the stats line tells you exactly how much overlap the lists had.

**Cleaning a log's unique error messages.** Grep output pasted here with SORT A→Z gives you a deduplicated, alphabetized inventory of distinct errors — a poor man's `sort | uniq` without leaving the browser.

**Deduplicating package lists.** Two `requirements.txt` files combined, deduped in original order, ready to commit.

## Edge cases & notes

- **Trim runs before comparison and output** — enabled, lines are compared *and emitted* trimmed. Disable it to treat `foo` and `foo ` as different lines and preserve exact whitespace.
- **Empty-line handling is explicit**: DROP EMPTY LINES removes them entirely; with it off, the first empty line is kept and later ones are treated as duplicates of it.
- **Sorting is locale-aware and numeric-smart** (`localeCompare` with `numeric: true`), so `line2` precedes `line10` — unlike plain byte sort.
- Windows (`\r\n`) and Unix (`\n`) line endings are both understood; output uses `\n`.
