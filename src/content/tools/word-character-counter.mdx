## What it does

Paste or type text and watch eight counters update on every keystroke: words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, lines, plus estimated reading and speaking time. No buttons to press, nothing uploaded.

## Examples

**Fitting a meta description.** SEO descriptions should stay under ~155 characters. Type it here and watch the CHARACTERS tile — no more counting by hand or publishing to find it truncated.

**Checking an abstract against a limit.** Conference limit: 250 words. Paste the draft; the WORDS tile answers instantly, and SENTENCES helps spot the 60-word monster sentence dragging your average up.

**Timing a conference talk.** A 2,600-word script shows exactly 20 minutes of speaking time at 130 wpm — right at the limit of a 20-minute slot, so you know to cut before rehearsal, not during.

## Edge cases & notes

- **Unicode:** counts are per UTF-16 code unit, the same measure JavaScript's `length` and most form `maxlength` attributes use. Some emoji and rare scripts count as 2.
- **Paragraphs** are blocks separated by blank lines; consecutive newlines without content don't create empty paragraphs.
- **Reading speed varies** — 200 wpm suits prose; expect ~150 for dense technical documentation and ~250 for light fiction.
- The counter handles book-length texts (500k+ characters) without lag; computation is a single linear pass.
